SOLARIT: RANDSEKTOR 07 0.20 - Intro Skip Fix
==============================

Problem:
- Intro could not reliably be skipped with ESC, Enter, Space or mouse click.
- The skip button could also appear visible while input was consumed elsewhere.

Fix:
- Intro skip handling moved to _input(), which runs before GUI consumption.
- _unhandled_input() no longer owns intro skip handling.
- Skip button explicitly receives mouse input, keyboard focus and high z-index.

Expected behavior:
- ESC skips intro.
- Enter skips intro.
- Space skips intro.
- Left-click anywhere skips intro.
- Clicking "ÜBERSPRINGEN / ESC" skips intro.
